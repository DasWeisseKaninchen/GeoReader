About GeoReader v1.0

GeoReader is a completely free, ad‑free tool that teaches you how to read real‑world signs written in non‑Latin scripts: Cyrillic (Russian, Ukrainian, Serbian, Kazakh, Bulgarian and more), Greek, Hebrew and Georgian.

🚏 Practice Mode: Pick a country and test your skills on realistic road signs. Match pairs and transliterate location names into the Latin script.

🧭 Learn Mode: Discover every letter through IPA-style sound samples and native audio for real place names. All in-game signs are Python-generated to mirror local standards - exactly what you will face in Practice mode.

🏅 Progress Tracking: Badges, per-script percentages, and your overall mastery are automatically saved directly on your device.

🔒 100% Offline & Private: Everything runs locally. No internet connection is required, and zero data leaves your machine.

Welcome! 👋

GeoReader is a tool designed to help you learn how to read real‑world signs — in Cyrillic (Russian, Ukrainian, Serbian, Kazakh, Bulgarian and more), Greek, Hebrew and Georgian — using real places and signs as context.

🧭 Learn — explore every letter, IPA‑style pronunciation samples and real place names with native pronunciation audio.

🚏 Practice — read road signs made to resemble real local styles and test your reading skills.

Your progress is saved automatically on this device — no account, no cloud.

Hebrew is unicameral — there are no uppercase / lowercase letters. Five letters use a final form at the end of a word: ךכ (כי) , םמ (מי) , ןנ (ני) , ףפ (פי) , ץצ (צדי) .
ℹ️ Georgian (Mkhedruli) is unicameral — there are no separate uppercase/lowercase letters.
